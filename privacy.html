Privacy Policy — Transparent PNG

Cut out image subjects and make backgrounds transparent in a Chrome side panel — Capture element or Upload image, processed on your device.

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Summary. Your pictures, processing jobs, and outputs are handled on your computer by the extension. We do not run a cloud matting service, do not sell your data, and do not use it for advertising. Page access and image fetching happen only when you start Capture or Upload — not as silent background monitoring.

1. Information We Collect

To provide background removal and export, the extension may handle:

  • Images you process — pixels from a file you upload (PNG, JPEG, or WebP) or from a page element you select with Capture element.
  • Processing state — in-progress job metadata, session flags for the side panel (for example picker or panel UI state), and short-lived buffers in extension memory or IndexedDB while a job runs or until you clear it.
  • Local UI preferences — small values in Chrome extension storage (for example whether an onboarding hint was dismissed).
  • Web page access (user-initiated only) — when you open the side panel, run Capture element, or use related actions, the extension may read the active tab, inject the picker overlay, and fetch image bytes from URLs on that page only for that action to build your cutout.
  • Exports you trigger — when you tap Done, the extension may write transparent.png (or a uniquified name) to your Downloads folder and copy PNG data to the clipboard using Chrome’s download and clipboard APIs.

We do not receive your images on developer-operated servers. The bundled segmentation model (ONNX, shipped inside the extension package) runs locally via WebGPU or WASM in the panel.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information is used only to operate features you request:

  • Show the side panel, progress, and Done export.
  • Run local flat-background cleanup or on-device ML matting for your image.
  • Resolve Capture targets (for example grid tiles or image URLs) when you pick an element.
  • Enforce product rules (for example blocked stock hosts or system pages) with in-panel errors.

We do not use your data for advertising, interest-based profiling, or marketing analytics.

3. Data Storage, Security & Network

On your device

Job buffers, pending uploads, and recent results may be stored in extension memory, IndexedDB, or chrome.storage (local or session) on your device. Uninstalling the extension removes extension storage; files you already saved to Downloads or elsewhere remain until you delete them.

The extension package includes model weights and ONNX Runtime Web libraries loaded from the extension bundle — not from a developer-controlled inference API.

Side panel (sidePanel)

The side panel is the main UI for Capture, Upload, processing, and Done. It runs as part of the extension, not as a remote website inside the panel.

Capture & host access (host_permissions)

Broad host permission is required because user content can appear on many sites. The extension does not monitor browsing in the background. Access is tied to your use of Capture, the picker, and related fetches while a job is active, as described on the Chrome Web Store listing and in the panel.

Third-party sites (not the developer)

During Capture, your browser may request images from the website you are viewing (for example a CDN). Those requests go between your browser and that site under their policies — we do not proxy your images through our servers.

HTTPS documentation

Opening this privacy or welcome page uses a normal HTTPS request to GitHub Pages (static text only; no image upload).

We do not operate a backend that stores your pictures. If our data practices change after release, we will update this policy and the store listing before the change takes effect.

4. Limited Use Disclosure

Use of information received through Chrome extension APIs follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy (including Limited Use requirements and collection limited to what is necessary for the extension’s disclosed single purpose):

  • Allowed use — only to provide or improve transparent-background cutout, Capture, Upload, and Done export (the extension’s single purpose).
  • Allowed transfer — we do not transfer your images to third parties for processing. Limited exceptions: legal requirements, security abuse investigations, or as part of a merger or sale with prior user notice where required.
  • Prohibited advertising — we do not use or transfer user data for personalized or interest-based ads.
  • Prohibited human reading — we do not routinely read your image content. Limited exceptions: your explicit support request, security investigation, or legal obligation.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Limited involvement of third parties:

  • GitHub Pages — hosts this welcome and privacy documentation (static pages; the extension does not send your images to GitHub).
  • Websites you capture — when you use Capture, image hosts you already visit may serve bytes to your browser; that is outside our control and not a transfer to us.

We do not use advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or tracking pixels in the extension UI.

6. Your Rights: Access & Deletion

  • Access — your output is shown in the panel and saved where you choose (Done → Downloads / clipboard).
  • Deletion — close or reset the panel job, clear browser data for the extension, or uninstall the extension to remove local extension storage. Delete exported files on disk separately.
  • Opt-out of web capture — use Upload image only with files from your disk; do not use Capture element.

7. Welcome Page (First Install)

When configured in a release, the extension may open our welcome page in a new tab on first installation:

https://promoseptika-sudo.github.io/transparent-png-pages/welcome.html

8. Permissions Reference

  • activeTab — access the tab where you start Capture.
  • tabs — find the website tab for capture and panel coordination.
  • scripting — inject the picker when you start Capture.
  • storage — save small local UI and session state.
  • sidePanel — main Capture / Upload / Done UI.
  • clipboardWrite — copy the processed PNG when you use Done.
  • downloads — save the processed PNG when you use Done.
  • offscreen — clipboard helper document when needed for copy.
  • webNavigation — coordinate picker lifecycle with navigation events.
  • host_permissions (http/https) — user-initiated Capture and content scripts for the picker on pages you use; not for background site surveillance.
